Week 0

22 Jul — 28 Jul 2026

Orientation & Personal Development

Week 0 focused on understanding personal strengths, improving self-awareness, and developing the right mindset before beginning the ProtoSem journey. The activities encouraged reflection, communication, and continuous learning.

Activities Done

  • 16 Personalities Test: Completed the MBTI personality assessment to understand my personality type, communication style, strengths, weaknesses, and preferred way of working in a team.
  • Zen Pencils Reflection: Read and reflected on motivational comics from Zen Pencils that emphasized perseverance, passion, discipline, creativity, and maintaining a growth mindset.

Topics Learned

  • Importance of self-awareness in personal and professional growth
  • Different personality traits and their impact on teamwork and communication
  • Growth mindset, perseverance, and continuous improvement
  • The value of reflection and learning from inspirational stories

Key Takeaways

  • Understanding my personality helps me collaborate effectively with others.
  • A positive mindset and consistent effort are essential for long-term success.
  • Self-reflection is an important habit for both personal and technical development.

Resources Used

  • 16Personalities Personality Assessment
  • Zen Pencils Inspirational Comics

Reflection

Week 0 helped me understand myself better and develop a positive mindset before starting the ProtoSem program. These activities strengthened my confidence, teamwork skills, and motivation for the weeks ahead.

Week 1

29 Jul — 04 Aug 2026

Getting Oriented: Foundations of Mechatronic Systems

Mapped out how mechanical, electrical, and software layers interact inside a typical mechatronic system, and set up the tools used to document progress every week.

Topics Learned

  • Core building blocks of a mechatronic system: sensors, actuators, controllers
  • Basics of signal flow between analog and digital domains
  • Overview of PLC scan cycles and ladder logic structure

Key Takeaways

  • Good documentation habits early on make later debugging far easier
  • Control system thinking applies across robotics, automotive, and manufacturing
  • Small, testable modules beat one large monolithic design

Resources Used

  • Department lecture notes — Introduction to Mechatronics
  • Siemens PLC fundamentals reference guide

Reflection

A strong first week — the biggest shift was learning to see a machine as a layered system rather than a single object. Next week I want to go deeper into sensor selection for the vending machine project.
